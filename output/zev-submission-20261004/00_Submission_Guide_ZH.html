可复制的英文项目文案

一句话介绍 对应实际必填字段

Zev is a shopping agent that buys within user-defined spending limits, asks about exceptions, and blocks purchases that break hard rules.

长版项目说明 供其他场合备用 表单没有此独立字段

Zev helps young shoppers delegate everyday restocking without giving an AI unrestricted access to money. A user signs a mandate defining product scope, spending caps, purchase count, expiry and conditions that require review. The agent interprets the request and compares the full basket across simulated merchants. Deterministic rules decide whether to allow, ask or deny. A HK$138 purchase can complete within a HK$150 cap, while a HK$158 basket including delivery is blocked. Human confirmation cannot override a hard refusal. The server settlement implementation rebuilds the decision context and rechecks the rules, with conditional writes inside one PostgreSQL transaction. The interface connects the request, mandate version, candidates, rule checks and receipt. This hackathon prototype uses simulated payments and authentication. Live FPS or Tap & Go integration, production identity checks and server-enforced address cooling remain future work.

补充视觉说明 供附件描述备用

Supplemental visuals include a product overview, annotated English UI screenshots covering mandate creation, ALLOW, REVIEW, DENY and the audit trail, an implementation diagram, and a Zev character-state design board. Screenshots depict the prototype; payments and authentication are simulated.

供评委快速体验

Open the demo, inspect a mandate, view an allowed HK$138 purchase, then the HK$158 refusal under the HK$150 cap. Inspect the REVIEW example and its expiry, then open the records to see the linked rule checks and receipt. Payments and authentication are simulated.
